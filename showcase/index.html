What it is

A free spotlight for music that helps people feel something.

The Showcase is a free, hand-picked spotlight for independent artists who use AI tools to bring their own words and stories to life.

It's for music that helps people heal, feel seen, or just feel something. MoBetta5150 Music believes AI can open doors for creators facing real-life challenges, and says so openly.

Showcase rules

Free, honest, and the artist stays in charge.

  • It's free to be featured. No pay-to-play, paid playlist spots, or "guaranteed plays."
  • Artists must own or have the rights to share their songs. Their AI tool's terms must allow it too.
  • AI use is disclosed on every feature.
  • Lyrics and credit belong to the artist.
  • Artists can ask to be removed at any time. Mo removes them promptly.
  • No hateful, harassing, or copied content. No imitating real artists' voices.
